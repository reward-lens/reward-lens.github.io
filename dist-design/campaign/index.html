The honest engineering note

Eight cards came back inconclusive for engineering reasons, not scientific ones: two hit a permission error in subject resolution, and six were missing an intermediate because an arc never ran or its shard never merged. The campaign runbook can re-run them against the same frozen specs. Eight other cards were frozen under explicit low-power acceptances, recorded in the freeze manifest before any evidence existed; each affected card page discloses its power and minimum detectable effect. The full accounting is on the method page.
